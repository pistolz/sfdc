## 1. Brief
- **Logline:** Gus owns the worst motel in America. A 5-star critic arrives in 30 days. Under 3 stars, it gets bulldozed. Viewers vote what he fixes each day.
- **Audience:** everyone (gross-out comedy + satisfying fixes), 13–45, global English.
- **Promise of each episode:** one gross problem → your vote → a fix (that may go hilariously wrong) → stars move.
- **KPI:** average watch time > 50 % (current channel: 1–2 s), sends, follows per 1k views.
- **Meta summary we aim for:** "This animated series follows Gus, owner of the worst motel in America, who has 30 days before a 5-star critic's visit; viewers vote what he fixes first."

## 2. Bible (summary — full art references in gate 2)
**Gus** — 54, owner. Big round belly, stained white tank top, open Hawaiian shirt, 5-day stubble, comb-over, one gold tooth, unlit cigar, flip-flops with socks. Lazy-eyed, deadpan, secretly cares.
**Margot Fields** — the critic. Never seen in full: only her heels, sunglasses, pen clicking. Mystery = suspense.
**The Roach** — one recurring cockroach (named by viewers later), Gus's only friend, breaks the 4th wall.
**The Golden Palm Motel** — 1970s roadside motel, desert edge, flickering neon palm, kidney pool turned swamp.

**Art direction — "the shine" recipe (what makes it look premium, not cheap):**
1. Flat vector shapes with **2-tone cel shading + soft gradient** on every surface.
2. **Rim light** (thin bright edge) on characters and props, opposite the key light.
3. **Specular highlights** on everything glossy (gold tooth, pool water, tiles, roach shells, eyes).
4. **Bloom/glow** on every light source (neon, lamps, phone screen), light shafts with floating dust.
5. **Contact shadows + ambient occlusion** under every object (nothing floats).
6. **Depth of field**: background slightly blurred, foreground props crisp.
7. **Hand-drawn touch**: variable-width ink outline with slight wobble + subtle paper grain.
8. **Grade**: warm sodium-orange night vs. teal shadows; vignette; render at 2× and downscale (crisp "4K" feel).
9. **Type**: chunky rounded display font, white with 6 px dark stroke + soft shadow, keyword in gradient yellow; words pop with overshoot.
10. **HUD** (always on): `DAYS LEFT 30 · ★☆☆☆☆ · GUESTS 0` in glossy pill badges with inner highlight.

**Sound palette (modern, no 60s cartoon clichés):** crisp foley (desk bell, fly buzz, roach skitter, squelch), airy whooshes on cuts, sub-bass drop on stakes, short riser into reveals, soft UI pops for text/HUD, one comic sting max per episode. Music: funky lo-fi bass groove ~96 BPM, ducked under VO, cut to silence before punchlines.

**Voice direction:** male narrator, **dry nature-documentary deadpan** (David-Attenborough-meets-sitcom). Average ~170 wpm but **varies**: fast on lists, slow + pause before punchlines, drop pitch on stakes, smile in the voice on the CTA. Gus never speaks words: only grunts/sighs (SFX).

## 3. Script — Day 1 (≈33 s, loops back to shot 1)
Marks: `/` short pause · `//` long pause · **bold** = stressed word · ↑ rising · ↓ falling.

1. "This is the **worst** hotel / in America." ↓
2. "One star. // On a **good** day."
3. "Meet **Gus**. / The owner."
4. "And in **thirty** days… // a five-star **critic** is coming to stay." ↑
5. "If she gives less than **three** stars…"
6. "…it gets **bulldozed**." ↓ (silence after)
7. "So Gus needs help. / **Your** help."
8. "Here's what we're working with." (fast)
9. "The lobby: roaches. / **Lots** of roaches."
10. "The bathroom… // we don't talk about the bathroom."
11. "The pool: / currently a **swamp**."
12. "Day one. One fix. **You** choose." ↑
13. "Comment **ROACHES** or **TOILET**. / Most-liked comment decides tomorrow."
14. "And trust me… // Gus is **not** doing this alone." (smile)
