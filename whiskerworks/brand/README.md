# WhiskerWorks brand

**Name:** WhiskerWorks  
**Tagline:** *Games with nine lives.*  
**Positioning:** an independent studio making small, replayable games (iPhone, Android), free to play, plus live video series the audience plays from the comments.

## Idea

Cats feel their way with their **whiskers** and get **nine lives** to keep trying. That is the studio's
promise: games tuned to the player, made to be replayed. The **Works** is the workshop: handmade, small batch.

The logo hides a detail: **the cat's mouth is a lowercase "w"**.

## Logo

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `logo/mark.svg` | Primary mark: ink tile, cream cat, marmalade whiskers. App icon, avatar, favicon. |
| `logo/mark-mono.svg` | One-colour version (`currentColor`): engraving, merch, watermarks. |

The wordmark is set live in **Unbounded**: "Whisker" at weight 600 and "**Works**" at 800, tracking −0.035em.

- Minimum size: 16 px (favicon); the eyes and "w" stay readable from 24 px.
- Clear space: one ear height on every side.
- Never stretch it, recolour the whiskers, or put the mark on a busy photo without its tile.

All tokens live in `packages/brand/tokens.css` and are shared by every site.

## Type

- **Unbounded** (display): headlines, numbers, labels in caps with +0.14em tracking.
- **Figtree** (body): everything you read.

Both are open-source (SIL OFL) and self-hosted through Fontsource: no Google Fonts requests and no GDPR issue.

## Voice

Short sentences. Warm, a little cheeky, never cute for the sake of it. Concrete promises
("Ten seconds to learn.") rather than adjectives. Talk to one player, not "users".

## Graphic language

- 2 px ink outlines and a hard offset shadow ("sticker" style) on cards and buttons.
- Buttons are pills with a coloured bottom shadow that compresses when pressed.
- A marmalade highlighter stroke under one key word per headline at most.
- Dark "night" sections for anything live (series, stats), with a moon-yellow kicker.
