          How it works

          1. Comment your football club on the latest episode.
          2. 1 club = 1 stand. The first comment for a club founds its stand (500 seats) and makes you its founder.
          3. Every comment after that adds 100 seats to the stand, and you get your own seat with your name on it.
          4. More seats level the stadium up: new tiers, a roof… all the way to 100,000 seats.

          Want your name off the stadium? Message us and we'll hide it at the next update.
