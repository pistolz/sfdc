Slide through. Hear the clack behind you. Keep climbing.

Snip! is a calm, endless arcade game played with one thumb. A yellow drop climbs a blue corridor on its own. Pairs of coral blades wait open ahead of it; slide left or right anywhere on the screen to slip through each gap. The moment you are through, the two blades snap shut behind you with a clean clack and a tiny click in your hand, and the closed pairs pile up below the drop like a ladder: the path you just made. Pair after pair, without end.

### One gesture, one rule

- Slide left or right anywhere: the drop follows your thumb directly, no inertia, no button, no timer, no lives
- Only one thing ends a game: touching a blade. Touching the walls never does
- The blades always close behind you, never in front of you
- The pace never speeds up and the gaps never shrink: only their positions change, so your thumb always does the same easy work
- A game ends? A new one starts by itself a moment later, no screen in between
- Every layout is checked so that a gentle correction is always enough

### Relax and find the rhythm

- A soft rush of air while you climb, one clean clack at every pass, always at the same pitch, a dry tick when you touch a blade. No music
- A small vibration at every clack, nothing else (Settings can turn it off)
- Score: one point per pair passed, nothing else on the screen
