Hold to pour. Release to stop. Don't spill a drop!

Honey Pour is a cozy sort puzzle. Pour flower honeys from jar to jar until every jar holds a single honey, filled right up to the golden line. Then the lid snaps shut: Perfect Pour!

### Every honey flows differently

Mint and Forget-me-not honey run fast. Lavender and Buckwheat honey are thick and keep trickling a little after you let go. Creamy Cotton honey barely moves. Learn each flow, time your release, and fill every jar exactly to the line.

### Simple rules

- Pour only onto the same honey, or into an empty jar
- A jar with one honey up to the golden line is sealed
- Seal every jar to win the level
- Overflow a jar and you lose one of your 3 drops
Levels are fixed, with no luck involved: if you spill, try again and pour smarter.

### New challenges as you go

- Locked jars that open when you seal other jars
- Frozen jars that thaw as you keep pouring
- Mystery honey hidden under a wax seal
- Hard and Super Hard levels for the best beekeepers

### Handy boosters

- Extra Jar: one more empty jar when space runs out
- Undo: take back your last pour
- Warm Honey: for a few seconds, honey stops right at the golden line
- Honey Spoon: scoop the top honey out of any jar

### Grow your hive

300 handcrafted levels, then endless new ones. Every level you win earns honey pots: use them to build and decorate the 8 rooms of your own hive, and come back every day for a daily gift.

### Made for everyone

- Flower labels on every honey for colour-blind players
- English and French
- Vibration, sound and music you can switch off
- Game Center leaderboards (optional): total stars and levels completed
- Optional sign-in with Apple or Google to back up your progress in the cloud
