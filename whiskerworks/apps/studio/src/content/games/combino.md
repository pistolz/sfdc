Slide golden dominoes onto a bright hexagon board. Every zone has its own rule. Make them all true, and collect colourful mosaics.

Combino is a calm logic puzzle. Each domino has two hexagon halves with 0 to 6 dots. Drag a domino onto two free cells, tap it to turn it, and fill the whole board so that every zone respects its badge.

### The rules speak for themselves

- 8: the dots in the zone add up to 8
- =: every cell of the zone has the same number of dots
- ≠: every cell has a different number of dots
- ＞3 and ＜3: every cell has more, or fewer, dots than the number
- Even and Odd: every cell is even (0, 2, 4, 6) or odd (1, 3, 5)
- Crown: the crowned cell must be strictly the highest of its zone
A zone gets a check mark as soon as it is full and true. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by logic, never by luck.

### Collect mosaics

Every new puzzle you solve adds a shiny candy-coloured tile to your current mosaic. Ten tiles complete a mosaic, with a little celebration, and each one has its own pattern and colours. Your daily puzzles build a mosaic of the month, and all your mosaics wait in your collection.

### 160 levels in 4 worlds

Classic, Mint, Lagoon and Galaxy. Each world brings new rules, introduced one at a time, and the boards grow from 6 to 30 cells.

### Play your way

- Infinite mode: endless new puzzles in 4 difficulties
- Daily challenge: three new puzzles every day (easy, medium, hard), the same for everyone, with your time shown after you solve them and a daily streak
- Game Center leaderboards (optional): total stars, Infinite puzzles solved and daily times, with your "top %" when Game Center data is available
- Rule Album: one card per rule, each with a real, sourced fact about mosaics, ceramic tiles or dominoes
- Undo is free and unlimited; 3 free hints every day
- No timer on screen, no lives, no penalty
- Night mode and board styles: Sunset, Candy, Pearl (cosmetic only)
- Your puzzle in progress is saved if you leave
- Optional sign-in with Apple or Google to back up your progress in the cloud
