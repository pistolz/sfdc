Hold the tap. Release on the line. Pour it perfect!

Honey Bar is a cozy time-management game. You run a little honey bar in a giant garden, and insect customers line up at your counter. Each one orders a glass of flower honey in layers: rose at the bottom, mint in the middle, golden on top... Read the order, pour each layer, add the topping and serve before their patience runs out.

### A real pouring skill

Hold a honey tap to pour, release to stop. Every honey flows at its own speed: mint runs fast, lavender is slow, creamed honey barely moves and keeps trickling after you let go. A glowing line shows where each layer must stop. Stop right on it for a PERFECT pour and a big tip. Too low? Top it up. Too high? Too late!

### Quirky customers

Lady Ladybug, elegant Mr. Butterfly and his four-layer orders, patient Sir Snail, Mr. Beetle who is always in a hurry, ants who order two glasses at once, an indecisive guest who changes the order while you pour, and the Queen Bee herself, who only accepts perfection.

### Upgrade your bar

Spend your tips on quicker taps, a warming plate that makes honey flow more smoothly, bigger glasses, comfier seats for more patience, a bigger counter with more seats, and cozy decorations.

### Travel from garden to garden

Sunny Meadow, Provence, Rose Valley, Kyoto Garden and the Royal Hive: 100 levels, 8 flower honeys, new customers and new scenery in every garden. Then keep the bar open in Honey Rush, an endless mode with every honey.

### Toppings and boosters

Finish each glass with honeycomb, pollen, royal jelly or a golden lid. When the counter gets busy:
- Slow Pour: every honey flows at half speed for 10 seconds
- Perfect Guide: the line glows and helps you stop on it for one order
- Patience Tea: refills every customer's patience
- +15 s: a little extra time on the clock

### Made for everyone

- Flower icons on every honey for colour-blind players
- 3 daily goals and a 7-day daily gift
- English and French
- Vibration, sound and music you can switch off
- Game Center leaderboards (optional): total stars and levels completed
- Optional sign-in with Apple or Google to back up your progress in the cloud
