Purrfect Stack is the game we play to wind down. You get a basket of cats, one at a time, and a tower that keeps asking for more. There is no clock and nothing chasing you: just the satisfying click of a cat that lands exactly where you wanted.

Every morning there is a new Daily Tower with the same basket for everyone, so your best height is directly comparable with your friends'.
