Hungry bees, a pixel picture, and one simple rule: they eat it from the bottom up.

Tap a crate of bees and it flies into your hive. One by one, its bees nibble the closest bottom cell of their color, and the picture disappears bite by bite. Clear every cell to win.

### Simple rules, real thinking

- Tap the front crate of a column: it moves into a free cell of the hive
- Each bee eats one cell of its own color
- Only the bottom cell of each column can be eaten: glowing cells are the ones you can eat now
- A crate with nothing to eat waits and blocks its hive cell, so choose your order well
- Hive full and nobody can eat? Continue with one extra hive cell, or replay
Every level can be won without boosters. No timer, no lives, no penalty.

### 200 levels in 5 worlds

Meadow, Orchard, Bakery, Seaside and Starry Night. Each world brings one new idea: hidden crates, twin crates, honey-glazed cells that take two bites, and locks opened by keys.

### Play your way

- Infinite mode: endless new pictures in 4 difficulties
- Daily Picture: three new pictures every day (small, medium, large), the same for everyone, with your time shown after you clear them and a daily streak
- Game Center leaderboards (optional): total stars, Infinite levels won and daily times, with your "top %" when Game Center data is available
- Album: every picture you clear joins your collection, plus 10 real, sourced facts about bees
- 4 boosters to help when you want: Extra Cell, Grabber, Shuffle and Tornado
- Hive themes (cosmetic only), night mode and a color-blind mode with a symbol for every color
- Speed x2 whenever you like
