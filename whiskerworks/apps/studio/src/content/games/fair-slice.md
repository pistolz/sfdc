Cakes on the table. Plates waiting. Only a few knives.

Fair Slice is a calm, cozy logic puzzle seen from above. Every plate asks for a number of slices and you have a small reserve of knives: cut the cakes, slide whole pieces onto the plates and give everyone exactly their share. No crumbs, nothing left on the table.

### One rule: every plate exact, nothing left

- Tap a cake to place a knife: the cut snaps to the cake's lines, no precision needed, then press CUT
- One knife = one cut = one piece split in two. Knives are counted
- Slide whole pieces onto the plates: a plate turns gold when it is exact, soft red when it gets too much
- Unlimited Undo (an undone cut gives its knife back), free restart, no timer during levels, no game over

### 5 worlds, 100 levels

Teatime, Birthday, Brunch, Picnic and Banquet. Each world adds one new idea: several cakes and three plates where nothing may be left, strawberry and chocolate zones with guests who ask for "no strawberry" or "at least one chocolate", cuts that go through two cakes at once, "in one piece" and "never the crust", and banquets with four plates where solutions become rare. Every level is checked to be solvable and has up to 3 stars: save a knife to earn the third one.
