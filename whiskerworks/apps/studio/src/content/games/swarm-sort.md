Draw a path. Guide the swarm. Watch the garden bloom!

Swarm Sort is a cozy path puzzle. Touch a swarm of bees, slide your finger tile by tile across the hex garden and let go on a flower of the same colour. The swarm follows your glowing pollen trail and pollinates the flower. When a flower has all the bees it needs, it bursts into bloom and frees up space on the board.

### Think before you fly

Swarms block each other and rocks block the way. Park a swarm in the little hive by the garden gate to clear a path, but the hive only has a few cells. Levels are fixed, with no luck involved: plan the order, then fly.

### Earn your stars

Solve a level in the fewest moves to earn 3 stars. Wrong paths cost nothing: the trail turns red and the swarm stays put.

### New surprises as you go

- Sleeping flower buds that wake up when other flowers bloom
- Wasps that move one step after each of your moves
- Mystery swarms whose colour stays hidden until they have room
- Tired swarms that can only fly so far
- Sprinklers that soak the grass every other move
- Hard and Super Hard levels for the best beekeepers

### Handy boosters

- Extra Cell: one more hive cell for the level
- Undo: take back your last move
- Shovel: remove a rock or a bush
- Queen Bee: the Queen leads a swarm straight to its flower

### Grow your garden

300 handcrafted levels, then endless new ones. Every win earns coins and nectar: use nectar to grow the 8 flower beds of your bee garden, from the Rose Bed to the Royal Orchard, and come back every day for a daily gift.

### Made for everyone

- Flower symbols on every swarm for colour-blind players
- Optional path help: reachable flowers glow when you touch a swarm
- English and French
- Vibration, sound and music you can switch off
- Game Center leaderboards (optional): total stars and levels completed
- Optional sign-in with Apple or Google to back up your progress in the cloud
