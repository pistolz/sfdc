A sofa too long. A door too narrow. Find the angle.

It Fits! is a calm, satisfying puzzle seen from above. A piece of furniture slides slowly toward a doorway on its own. You do only one thing: swipe anywhere to turn it. Find the right angle and it slips into the next room with a happy pop.

### One rule: turn it until it fits

- Swipe left or right anywhere on the screen to rotate the piece, smoothly, to the exact angle you want
- Soft parts squeeze: watch the sofa cushions compress as they rub the door frame... just enough
- Hard parts never bend: wood, metal, glass and stone need the right angle
- The door frame turns red where it rubs, so you always see what is wrong
- Too tight? The piece gets stuck, backs up a little and you adjust. No game over, no timer on screen

### 5 worlds, 75 handmade levels

Studio, Apartment, Hallway, Fragile and Staircase. Each world adds one new idea: L- and T-shaped corner sofas and desks, hallways with two doors where you turn again in between, fragile pieces like a grand piano with a lamp on top or an armchair with someone asleep in it, and staircase landings where the whole piece turns the corner. Every level has 3 to 5 pieces and up to 3 stars.
