Pack the nuts. Pour the honey. Perfect Jar!

Jar Packer is a calm packing puzzle. Every level shows a target jar with a pretty pattern: rings of almonds, stripes of pistachios, diagonals of hazelnuts. Nuts arrive one by one on a little conveyor. Drag each one into the glass jar to rebuild the pattern, piece by piece.

### Every nut has its shape

A peanut fills one cell, an almond stands two cells tall, a pistachio lies two cells wide, a walnut takes a 2x2 block and a cashew makes an L. Pieces never rotate, and gravity is on: a nut must rest on the bottom of the jar or on another nut. A wrong spot simply does not snap in, and it costs you nothing.

### Plan ahead

You can only take the nut at the front of the conveyor. Not the right moment? Park it on the 3-slot bench and use it later. Bench full and nothing fits? You are "Out of space!": undo, retry or add one more bench slot.

### Pour the honey

When the last nut clicks into place, tap Pour Honey and watch golden honey flow into every gap. Lid on, stars in, and your jar joins your shelf.

### Earn your stars

Use the bench as little as possible to earn 3 stars. No timer, no rush.

### 100 jars to collect

- 5 collections of 20 jars: Classics, Turkish Jars, Party Jars, Hexagon Jars and Royal Jars
- Endless Encore jars after level 100
- A Daily Jar, the same for every player, with a 7-day streak bonus
- A Daily Gift, and My Shelf, where every jar you pack is on display
- A new shelf decoration for every collection you complete

### Handy boosters

- Undo: take back your last move
- Extra Bench: one more bench slot for the level
- Peek: see 5 more nuts on the conveyor
- Swap: swap the front nut with any nut on the conveyor

### Made for everyone

- Colour-blind mode: a pattern on each kind of nut
- Ghost hints: the target pattern shown faintly inside the jar
- English and French
- Vibration, sound and music you can switch off
- Game Center leaderboards (optional): total stars and levels completed
- Optional sign-in with Apple or Google to back up your progress in the cloud
