The cake turns. You hold the piping bag.

Piping! is a calm, endless game played with one finger. A tiered cake spins on its stand at a steady pace; slide up or down anywhere on the lower half of the screen to keep the nozzle on the edge, and watch a twisted cord of cream land all around it. One full turn and the tier is done: it settles onto the cake below and the next one arrives. Tier after tier, without end.

### One gesture, one rule

- Slide to follow the edge: the nozzle follows your finger, no button, no timer, no lives
- Slightly inside, the cord gets messy. Slightly outside, it droops: that is the warning
- Stay clearly outside and the cream falls. Splotch. A new cake starts at once, no screen in between
- The cake never speeds up: only its shapes change, from circles to soft waves, scallops, hearts and rounded stars, never the same twice
- Every shape is checked so that following the edge always works and standing still never does

### Relax, the rewards come on their own

- Clean tiers turn the cord pearly, then golden, with a shine that runs along it
- The colours of the tiers change every 10 tiers
- When a game ends, a cherry lands on top
- Your tallest cake is rebuilt in 3D on the home screen, cord by cord
