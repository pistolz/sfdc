Where will it land?

Call It is a calm physics prediction puzzle. A ball waits at the top of a course of ramps, bumpers and cushions. Slide your marker along the finish strip to the exact spot where you think the ball will first touch it, then tap Drop it! and watch.

### One ball, one course, one prediction

- The whole course is visible before the drop: nothing hidden, no luck added
- Every object shows what it does: shiny bounces, matte absorbs, striped launches
- The same course always gives the same fall, on every device
- Your gap is always shown in centimeters, hit or miss
- Missed by a hair? So close! Tap Next challenge or Retry right away
- Watch the fall again in slow motion, with the full trajectory and every impact

### 60 handmade challenges in 4 worlds

Ramps, Bumpers & Cushions, Trampolines and Seesaws. Each world teaches one new object, then mixes them into trickier courses. Earn up to 3 stars per challenge and a bronze, silver or gold badge per world. Only your first prediction counts for stars; retrying is free practice.

### Play your way

- Daily Challenge: 5 new courses every day, the same for everyone, one counted prediction each, a daily score out of 1,000 and a daily streak
- Infinite: endless new courses in Easy and Medium, plus Hard as an optional purchase
- Sure? x2: feeling confident? Make your target zone half as wide to double your Daily Challenge points
- Hints: see the first bounce, 3 free every day
- Challenge a friend: share a picture of a course, without the answer
- Game Center leaderboards (optional): total stars, best Infinite streak and Daily Challenge score, with your "top %" when Game Center data is available
- No timer, no lives, no screen shake
- Night mode and left-handed mode
- Optional sign-in with Apple or Google to back up your progress in the cloud
