Vacuum everything... except the cat.

Not the Cat! is a calm, cozy puzzle. A little blue robot vacuum waits in a sunny living room full of crumbs, socks and toys, and a ginger cat is fast asleep on his cushion. Slide your finger to guide the robot and clean the whole room, without waking him up.

### One rule: don't make too much noise

- Every object you vacuum makes a sound: a crumb is tiny, a sock is louder, a pillow is a big BOOM
- The closer you are to the cat, the louder it sounds to him
- Furniture between you and the cat muffles the noise
- The noise gauge goes down by itself: waiting is a strategy
- The cat warns you: an ear twitches, then he stretches... at 5 notches he wakes up
- He wakes up, swats the robot, and you try again right away, for free. The cat is never hurt

### Grow, push, plan your order

The robot grows as the room gets cleaner and can swallow bigger things. Once it is big, it can push the sofa or the coffee table to build a wall of quiet between you and the cat. Clean far away first, grow, move the furniture, finish close to him.

### 80 handmade rooms in 4 homes

Studio, Apartment, Country House and Loft. Meet four cats who sleep differently: the ginger cat, the black cat, a kitten who sleeps lightly and an old grey cat who changes cushions halfway. Earn up to 3 stars per room.
