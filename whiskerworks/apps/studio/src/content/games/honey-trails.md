Link the bees. Grow the chain. Jump the levels!

Honey Trails is an endless bee puzzle. Put your finger on a bee and draw a free honey trail through bees of the same level. Lift your finger and the whole chain merges into one bigger bee, right where your trail ends.

### The longer the chain, the higher it goes

- 2 or 3 bees: +1 level
- 4 or 5 bees: +2 levels
- 6 bees or more: +3 levels
Every merge brings 2 new bees onto the board, so long chains climb faster AND free up space.

### Draw, don't cross

There is no grid: draw your trail anywhere inside the big honeycomb. Go around the other bees, never cross your own trail, and never cross the trail of your last move: it stays on the board for one turn, then dries. An invalid trail simply wobbles away, with no penalty.

### One board, no end

Start with three kinds of bees. Grow them level after level up to the golden Queen at level 11... and beyond, with a new golden ring for every level above. The game ends when there is no room for new bees or no pair can be linked any more.

### How far can you go?

- Game Center leaderboards (optional): highest level, best score and a daily leaderboard
- See where you stand among players on the leaderboard
- Daily Challenge: the same start and the same new bees for everyone, every day
- The Hive Family album: one card per level, each with a real fact about bees
- 4 cosmetic board themes
- One free undo per game, hints, and one continue per game
- Your game in progress is saved if you leave
