Drop. Sink. Merge. Golden honeycomb!

Nutty Drop is a calm physics puzzle. A little bee holds a nut above a jar full of honey. Slide your finger to aim, release to drop, and watch the nut sink slowly into the thick honey. When two identical nuts touch, they merge into a bigger one.

### Honey changes everything

Nuts never bounce: they glide down, settle softly and push their neighbours like spoons in syrup. A dotted line shows where each nut will land, so every drop is a real decision. Every merge sends a gentle wave through the jar that can trigger the next merge, and the next one.

### From peanut to golden honeycomb

Peanut, hazelnut, almond, pistachio, walnut, macadamia, giant cashew, Brazil nut, dried fig... and the golden honeycomb. Merge two honeycombs for a golden blast that frees up space.

### Don't cross the red line

If a nut stays above the red line for 2 seconds, the game is over. Keep calm, plan your stack and chase a new best score. Once per game, you can continue: the spoon lifts out the three highest nuts.

### A new challenge every day

Every day brings one special rule: a narrow jar, thick honey, a chain that starts at the hazelnut, big drops, a walnut hunt, or only 40 drops. The same challenge for everyone, and coins when you reach the goal.

### Collect pots and honeys

Unlock 12 pots and 8 honeys as your total score grows, or with coins. They are just for looks: every jar plays the same, so every score is fair.

### Handy boosters

- Shake: the whole jar settles a little
- Spoon: remove the nut of your choice
- Swap: switch the nut in hand with the next one
- Warm Honey: runnier honey for 10 seconds

### Made for short breaks

- Your game in progress is saved if you leave
- A daily gift to collect
- English and French
- Vibration, sound and music you can switch off
- Game Center leaderboards (optional): best score and total score
- Optional sign-in with Apple or Google to back up your progress in the cloud
