Draw one line. Bloom the whole garden.

Pollen Path is a calm one-line logic puzzle. Start on flower 1 and trace the bee's honey path from cell to neighboring cell, through the numbered flowers in order, until you reach the last flower. Every cell you cross bursts into bloom. The last flower only opens when the whole garden bed is in bloom.

### Simple rules, real deduction

- Start on flower 1, finish on the last flower
- Reach the numbered flowers in order: 1, 2, 3...
- Cover every cell, never the same cell twice
- Hedges: never cross a hedge
- Wind: leave a windy cell only in the direction of its arrow
- Bridges: cross each bridge twice, straight, once over and once under
Every level has exactly one solution, found by logic, never by luck. Slide back to unbloom a step, or touch any cell of your path to continue from there.

### 200 levels in 5 worlds

Flowerbed, Hedgerow, Windy Meadow, Bridges and The Hive, where the garden turns into hexagons. Each world brings one new idea, and the beds grow as you go.

### Play your way

- Infinite mode: endless new puzzles in 4 difficulties
- Daily Garden: three new puzzles every day (small, medium, large), the same for everyone, with your time shown after you solve them and a daily streak
- Game Center leaderboards (optional): total stars, Infinite puzzles solved and daily times, with your "top %" when Game Center data is available
- My Garden: every level you solve plants a flower in your own garden
- Album: 10 flower species to unlock, each with a real, sourced fact about bees and pollination
- Restart and going back are free and unlimited; 3 free hints every day
- No timer on screen, no lives, no penalty
- Night mode, left-handed mode and garden themes (cosmetic only)
- Your path in progress is saved if you leave
- Optional sign-in with Apple or Google to back up your progress in the cloud
