# videotry — Making "STUCK AT 5:58" videos (Instagram / TikTok / Shorts series)

> Everything you need to understand Episode 1 and produce the next ones yourself.
> Code: `reels-studio/` · Episode 1: composition `StuckAt558-Day1` (45 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps).

---

## 1. Video type (the format)

**"Day N" series of the "survival + HUD + rules" type**, inspired by two reference reels ("Forest of Infinite Wood" and "Pokémon World Day 1"), but with **100% original IP** (no Pokémon, no existing brand).

The format's DNA (the things that make it work):

| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| **HUD on top of the image** (day, clock, counters, countdown) | Gives the viewer "systems to watch": they keep watching to see the numbers change |
| **Numbered rules** ("RULE #1…") | They structure the series and stay pinned in the HUD, like the episode's memory |
| **A countdown** (100 nights) | Long-term stakes: there is a reason to follow the series |
| **2–3 word captions** with a yellow or red keyword | Readable without sound, keep the rhythm |
| **Fast, concrete voice-over** (numbers, short sentences) | Credibility + tension |
| **Hook in < 3 s** | Stops the scroll |
| **Cliffhanger + double participation loop** | Follow = your name in the story; comment = you decide what comes next |
| **Ideal length: 35–50 s** | 90 s is too long for a Reel |

## 2. Theme and universe: STUCK AT 5:58

**Pitch in one sentence:** *Time stopped at 5:58 in a town of 4,012 people. Every time you sleep, the world moves forward exactly one second. The 6:00 alarm is 100 nights away. And something else is awake.*

### The world: Dunmoor
- A small rainy British-style town. The town clock is frozen at **5:58:20 AM**.
- **The rain hangs in the air** like glass beads. A bolt of lightning is frozen above the clock tower.
- **4,012 inhabitants are frozen mid-stride**, eyes closed, sleepwalking toward work, phone in hand. Every phone shows **ALARM 6:00 AM**.
- Only **you** can move (the hero in the **yellow raincoat**). Your phone works, with no signal.

### The physics (the rules of the world; never break them)
1. **The tick:** every time you sleep, the world moves forward **1 second**. 5:58:20 → 6:00:00 = **100 nights**.
2. **What you push stays pushed:** walking through the rain leaves a **tunnel** (a footprint).
3. **Rain + pressure = glass:** packed drops fuse into glass that glows orange. That's what you build with.
4. **Sleepers keep walking:** at each tick, each one takes **1 step**.
5. **Something else is awake.** It only moves **while you sleep**. You see its tunnels (2 m tall). It isn't a sleeper.
6. **When the alarm rings (night 100), the town wakes up.** Nobody knows what happens to whoever is outside the glass.

### Rules (one or two per episode, max 3 visible in the HUD)
- Rule #1: **Never walk inside its tunnel.** (Day 1)
- Ideas for what follows: "Take nothing from a sleeper's hand", "Photograph the room before you sleep", "Never sleep facing the glass", "Count the steps every morning"…

**Script-writing rules (to reuse):**
- 105–125 words for about 45 s. Very short sentences, fragments. Present tense, second person ("you").
- Concrete numbers everywhere (4,000 / 6 A.M. / 100 nights / 2 metres / 3 sleepers / 3 steps).
- Write numbers **the way the voice should say them** ("five fifty-eight", "six A.M.").
- Every number spoken must be **shown in the HUD** (consistency = credibility).
- Structure: hook (3 s) → setup → stakes/countdown → mystery → rule → action/build → sleep + tick → twist → CTA.

## 4. Visual style and animations

### Image style
- **2D cartoon / indie game**: simple shapes, flat colors, soft shading, thick outlines, **big-head (chibi)** characters.
- Palette: **night blue + orange/amber** (street lamps, glass). Red is for danger only.
- Recurring hero: a **small character in a yellow raincoat** with the hood up (instantly recognizable).
- No realism (rejected: it doesn't match the references).

### Text / captions (measured on the references)
- Font **Anton** (`public/fonts/Anton.ttf`), 112 px, centered at **y = 1291** (67% of the height).
- 8 px black outline + soft drop shadow.
- Colors: white `#FAF9F9`, **yellow `#F5D65B`** = positive/goal, **red `#DC5F52`** = threat.
- Animation: first frame at 50% opacity, scale 0.92 → 1 over 3 frames, **hard cut on exit**, 1-frame gap between captions.
- In the code: `[Y]word[/Y]` = yellow, `[R]word[/R]` = red.

### HUD
- Top left (x 64, y 262): red dot blinking **17 frames on / 13 off**, "DAY 1", frozen big clock (the seconds twitch slightly), line "TOWN OF DUNMOOR · YOU: 6:14 AM" (your watch keeps running).
- Top right: **SAFE x / 4012** (pop on each +1), then the **7-segment** "ALARM IN … NIGHTS" mini card.
- Rules: pill with a red "RULE #1" tag and white text, under the clock (stack spacing 64 px).
- **Safe zones:** nothing important above y = 250 or below y = 1500 (Instagram's interface covers those areas).

### Animations (all in Remotion, nothing frame-by-frame)
- Slow camera moves (Ken Burns) on each image: zoom 1.0 ↔ 1.3, eased.
- **Frozen rain**: drops that do NOT fall, they only glint.
- Countdown card: scale-in 0.8 → 1 (4 frames), then **docks** into the corner (7 frames, scale 0.4).
- Rule card: slides in from the right with overshoot (11 frames), karaoke-style word lighting, docks into the HUD (13 frames).
- Sleep: black **eyelids** close and then open; white **flash** at the tick.
- Final cards: spring pop, chevron bounce.

## 6. Making the next episode (Day 2, Day 3…), step by step

### 0. Install (once)
```bash
cd reels-studio
npm install
pip install edge-tts soundfile numpy scipy faster-whisper
python3 tools/sfx/render_bank.py        # regenerates the sounds (wav files are not in git)
```
(On your machine, delete `Config.setBrowserExecutable(...)` in `remotion.config.ts` so Remotion uses its own Chrome.)

### 1. Write the script
- `episodes/stuck-at-558/day2.txt`, 105–125 words, following the structure in section 3.
- Check consistency: clock (Day 2 = **5:58:21 → 22**), countdown (**99 → 98**), SAFE, rules already established.

### 2. Generate the voice
```bash
python3 tools/tts_pipeline.py --text episodes/stuck-at-558/day2.txt --out /tmp/vo2 \
  --engine edge --voice en-US-GuyNeural --rate +22%
python3 tools/tighten_pauses.py /tmp/vo2 public/audio/ep2 --max 0.24 --keep "twenty-two.=0.7"
```
→ prints the duration (target 38–42 s) and creates `public/audio/ep2/words.json`.
To see each word's timing: `python3 -c "import json;[print(w['w'],w['s']) for w in json.load(open('public/audio/ep2/words.json'))]"`

### 3. Generate the images (4 to 6)
Base prompt (always keep this style sentence word for word):
> *2D cartoon illustration in the style of an animated YouTube story channel / cozy indie game: clean simple shapes, flat colors with soft cel shading, thick soft outlines, chibi proportions with big heads. Vertical scene: … No text, no letters.*

Always mention: *small chibi hero in a bright yellow raincoat with the hood up*, *raindrops frozen in mid-air like shiny beads*, *deep blue night, warm orange lights*.
Format 9:16, export at 1080×1920 (procedure in `tools/IMAGE_PIPELINE.md`), save as `public/plates/d2_p1.jpg`, etc.
Tip: keep the main subject between y = 420 and 1100 (between the HUD and the captions).

### 4. Create the composition
- Copy `src/stuck558/Episode.tsx` → `src/stuck558/Day2.tsx`.
- Change: the `words.json` import, the **`T`** timeline (seconds, read from words.json), the **`CHUNKS`** (text shown + number of spoken words; the total must equal the voice's word count, otherwise you get an explicit error), the images in the `<Shot>` blocks, the HUD values (DAY 2, seconds, SAFE, countdown, rules), the SFX list.
- Add a `<Composition id="StuckAt558-Day2" …>` in `Root.tsx`.

### 5. Preview and render
```bash
npx remotion studio src/index.ts                 # live preview in the browser
npx remotion render src/index.ts StuckAt558-Day2 out/day2.mp4
ffmpeg -i out/day2.mp4 -c:v copy -af "loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5:LRA=7" -c:a aac -b:a 192k out/day2_final.mp4
```

### 6. Publishing (tips)
- Caption: one question + the choice ("OPEN or STAY? 👇"), hashtags `#StuckAt558 #Day2 #story`.
- **Pin a comment** that restates the vote.
- Note the answers (top comment, new followers) → they feed the next episode.

## 7. My vision for what comes next

### How participation works (it has to show on screen)
- **Follow = saved sleeper**: in the next episode, show **wrists with the names** of real followers or commenters (ask "comment WRIST if you want your name"), plus a **NAMED** counter in the HUD.
- **Comment = decision**: every episode ends with a binary choice. The next episode **opens on the result**: "61% said STAY. So we stayed."

### Day 2 (draft)
- **Hook:** *"Look at the glass. Five fingerprints. Her hands never left her phone."* (Something touched the glass, but it wasn't her.)
- Result of the vote (OPEN / STAY), shown with a percentage.
- Real names written on 3 wrists, NAMED counter.
- Rule #2: **"Photograph the room before you sleep"** (to spot what changed after the tick).
- Action: widen the wall, bring in 2 more sleepers (SAFE 3 → 5).
- Sleep → **5:58:22**, countdown **98**.
- Twist: in the morning's photo, a **4th sleeper** is standing inside, behind the glass… and you never brought them in.
- Choice: "WAKE HER or LOCK HER OUT?"

### Arc over 100 nights (outline)
| Nights | Phase | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | **Shelter** | Build the glass house, first rules, the tunnels get closer every night |
| 11–25 | **Map** | Exploring Dunmoor (church, school, hospital); the tunnels form a pattern on the map |
| 26–40 | **The other** | First direct trace of the "thing" (a silhouette caught in a photo); it **copies your rules** |
| 41–60 | **Survivors** | Another awake person shows up. Ally or the thing in disguise? (big audience vote) |
| 61–80 | **The lightning** | The frozen bolt gets closer to the tower with every tick; it will strike at night 90 |
| 81–99 | **Countdown** | Saving as many sleepers as possible (SAFE vs 4,012), the tunnels surround the glass |
| 100 | **6:00:00** | The alarm rings. The whole town wakes up… and the answer: the thing was **the town's 4,013th inhabitant**, the one nobody counted (seeded from Day 1 with "POP. 4,012"). |

### Recurring hooks for future episodes
- Open on **a detail that changed** since the last tick (visual "spot the difference" game).
- One new number per episode (steps, metres, fingerprints, %).
- Always end on: **tick + twist + choice + "DAY N+1"**.
